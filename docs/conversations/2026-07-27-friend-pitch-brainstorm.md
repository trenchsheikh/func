## ChatGPT's reconstructed summary (complete, as supplied by Phoenix)

### Purpose
Different presentation decks for different audiences (investors, developers,
promoters, event managers, designers) — one master deck (~100 slides),
shorter versions cut from it per audience.

### What is FUNC?
A central hub for nightlife/social experiences — clubs, bars, restaurants,
parties, festivals, private tables, events, promoters, venues, friends,
stories, planning, invitations, all in one place. Not another social network,
booking app, or maps app — the hub that replaces needing all of those.

### The map
Centre of the app. Google-Maps-quality polish, but live social information
instead of roads: venues, live activity, prices, entry costs, crowd density,
popularity, availability, possibly male:female ratio, recommendations.

### Opening animation
Eye logo opens → becomes the world → zooms into your location → nearby
events appear.

### Why people will use it
Removes the "what should we do tonight" problem — replaces checking
Instagram/TikTok/Google/WhatsApp/Eventbrite/club sites/promoter DMs/friends
with one app.

### Stories
Debated live in the conversation — concern they'd fail if underused,
countered that they make profiles more useful (see who's actually out).
Conclusion: include them, but as secondary to the map.

### Event discovery / swiping
Tinder-style swipe browsing for events/tables was proposed and directly
disputed in the same conversation ("nobody's going to use the swiping" vs.
"people are for sure going to use the swiping") — **left unresolved by the
speakers themselves**, not just under-specified.

### Group planning
Groups browse/vote/filter together (budget, age, distance, group size)
instead of endless "what should we do" group chat messaging.

### Account types
Standard user vs. promoter/event-manager accounts with different interfaces —
promoters get QR-code check-in tools, can swipe on requests for their
tables/events, don't have to cold-DM.

### Monetisation ideas floated
Paid promotion/boosted visibility for events; tokens (currency for
promotion, possibly tied to premium tiers); concierge tiers correlated to
voucher volume. None of these were resolved into a single model in the
conversation.

### Design language
Green, black, white; "bubbly and colourful" but with a "slick nightlife
vibe"; eye logo/motif carried through as the iconic visual anchor. Also
floated (jokingly, unresolved): a peacock or statue as a map marker for
tables that have bottle service.

### Calendar
Briefly floated — auto-scheduling/plan-to-calendar integration for
multi-date planning. Not developed further ("might not be good, but that
might be good").

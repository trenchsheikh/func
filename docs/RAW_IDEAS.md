# Raw ideas — everything, filtered or not

Added 2026-08-04 at Phoenix's direction. Separate from the tracker on
purpose: the tracker only holds ideas that have been through intake and
tiered. This file holds **everything Func-related that gets said or thought**,
including things that never make it into the prototype. Nothing gets left out
for being small, half-formed, or later rejected.

**Append, never delete or rewrite.** If an idea is superseded or dropped, add
a new entry saying so — don't erase the old one. This is the record of how
the thinking moved, not just where it landed.

Each entry: date, source, the idea in Phoenix's own terms (don't polish it
away), and status if known (`raw` / `superseded` / `in tracker` / `dropped`).

## 2026-08-07

Everything said in the prototype-rebuild session, in Phoenix's own terms.

- **"Func is just a bridge between you and the opportunities of the night."**
  Status: **adopted** — this is now the product line (Decision 012).
- **"no groups. forget about that. any group system might just create extra noise
  which deters people from wanting to use func."** Status: adopted as a Tier 0
  change; resolved as "groups are a number, not an object" (Decision 012).
- **"no voting on the app for things to do thats dumb - people use other apps to
  communicate."** Status: adopted — kills Call It / In-Nah / Plan Flag.
- **Traffic light profile system.** *"green = dtf (down to func) youre down to do
  anything and youre on the app to have fun. amber = using the app to check things
  out casually but not really ready to commit to anything. red = inactive and not out
  at all. Disco ball = premium account (super dtf) and gold disco ball = business
  account - havent fully decided on that but for this is fine."* Status: **adopted**,
  built. Note the business-account tier is explicitly not fully decided.
- **The pet / AI assistant.** *"micha and i were playing around with the idea of
  having a customisable pet that doubles as an ai assistant - a super helpful and
  intelligent assistant that is a genuinely impressive model but also fun and jumps
  around the screen with you and follows you on the map maybe - just a fun idea right
  now not complete."* Later specified as *"very cute and friendly disco creature."*
  Status: **in the prototype**, designed properly. Concept still open.
- **Opening animation.** Eye opens → iris becomes the globe → spins to SF → dive to
  your street. *"map animation opening is vital (the eye and the globe theme) because
  func.'s image is what will make it rise to the top immediately."* Status: adopted.
  The globe is permanent — pinch out any time to see every Func city.
- **The pitch runs a whole night:** *"starting off with early drinks/dinner then bar
  then club then after the club looking for a group of people hosting after party."*
  Status: adopted as the demo arc.
- **"like Hinge but quicker and for groups"** — said early, then contradicted by "no
  groups". Status: **contested**, resolved toward the swipe/browse page. Both
  positions preserved per the contested-idea rule.
- **Browse/swipe page:** *"a page where you can swipe for literally anything from
  cool dinner spots to after parties and hang outs. You set the filter and the app's
  future algorithm will recommend too based on age, profile type (traffic light
  profile system) and you can swipe through looking for things to do when you cant be
  bothered to use the map."* Status: adopted, built.
- **No time pressure.** On rejecting both a time-morphing map and a night timeline:
  *"i like neither because it means you have no commitment to the app - you dont feel
  tracked or rushed at all. I also like it feeling alive though. I think for now start
  with neither and then we can build up from there. The map just shows everything and
  you can filter or search if you like."* Status: adopted as a standing principle.
  The time-of-night map and the timeline are **deferred, not dropped** — he explicitly
  said "build up from there."
- **Filters:** *"EVERYTHING - a vibe category separation, distance, cost, group size,
  etc. all optional to add, all possible to specify."* Status: adopted.
- **Heat shown as glow only** — no numbers, no words on the map. Status: adopted.
  (I flagged the legibility risk; he chose pure light. Words live in the venue sheet.)
- **Profile:** photos + traffic light + bio with Instagram + ID verification.
  *"We can fine tune vouches another time once the app is built."* Status: vouches
  **deferred, not deleted**.
- **Friends:** normal requests, plus people you actually shared a table or
  after-party with arriving as pending. Mutuals visible around the map.
- **After-parties:** address revealed only on accept. Status: adopted, built.
- **Stories:** postable from anywhere, but posts genuinely from a venue get a
  verified mark and show on that venue. Status: adopted.
- **Also adopted this session:** niche local spots earning tokens when people visit,
  a night rating that feeds the map's accuracy rather than being a public review, and
  afters mode (the app changes personality after midnight).
- **Accessibility** means *"anyone can use it instantly"* + *"open to everyone"* —
  low barrier, free, no gatekeeping. Disability accessibility was offered and not
  selected; contrast and touch-target discipline applied as craft anyway.
- **Deferred, not dropped:** concierge subscription ladder, the paid-answers version
  of local knowledge, calendar integration, bottle-service/peacock map markers,
  Backing/vouches, the time-of-night map, the night timeline.
